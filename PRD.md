                                Target Users

Hostel Power Watch is primarily designed for students living in Babcock University hostels.

Primary Users

1. Hostel Students: Students who need access to current electricity information to plan their daily activities, charging schedules and academic work.

2. Designated Hostel Representatives: Individuals responsible for providing and updating the official electricity status of their assigned hostels.

Secondary Users

3. System Administrators: Individuals responsible for managing user accounts, assigning hostel representatives and overseeing the platform.


                           User Needs

1. Students need reliable and timely electricity updates.
2. Hostel representatives need a simple way to update electricity status.
3. Administrators need tools to manage the platform and monitor reported issues.


                             Core Problems Identified

The main problem is the lack of a reliable and timely way for hostel students to know the current electricity status.

This creates three major challenges:

1. Uncertainty: Students do not know when electricity will return after an outage.
2. Lack of Warning: Students receive no advance notification when electricity is about to go off.
3. Wasted Time: Students repeatedly check whether electricity has returned instead of being able to check its status remotely.

These challenges make it difficult for students to plan their daily activities, charge their devices, iron their clothes and complete academic tasks.

Hostel Power Watch aims to address these problems by providing students with accessible and timely electricity status updates.


                                            Research Methodology

The research for Hostel Power Watch focuses on understanding the electricity challenges experienced by students living in Babcock University hostels.

The proposed research methods include:

1. Interviews: Speaking with hostel residents to understand their experiences with unpredictable electricity supply and how they currently receive electricity updates.

2. Observation: Observing how students respond to power outages and how they determine when electricity has been restored.

3. Questionnaires: Gathering responses from students about the frequency of power interruptions, the challenges they experience and their opinions on a centralised electricity-status platform.

                                                 Information to Be Collected

- Students' experiences with unpredictable electricity.
- How students currently receive electricity updates.
- The effects of power interruptions on academic and personal activities.
- Students' opinions about receiving electricity updates through a digital platform.

                                                 Research Participants

The intended participants are students residing in Babcock University hostels, particularly those who regularly experience electricity interruptions.
